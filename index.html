Meet the Founder

Every next chapter deserves a thoughtful beginning.

Hi, I'm Sophia, founder of Nest & Next Seattle. I started Nest & Next with a simple belief: life's transitions should feel less overwhelming and more manageable.

Whether you're looking to organize your home, helping a parent downsize, preparing a home for sale, or navigating a move to a smaller space, I understand that there's much more involved than simply packing boxes.

My approach is personal, organized, and hands-on. I help bring structure to the process by organizing spaces, coordinating moving logistics, managing projects, and making sure the little details don't become big headaches.

Most importantly, I want every client and family to feel supported, respected, and confident throughout the process. Because every next chapter deserves a thoughtful beginning.
