Ways we can help

Support for your home, your move, and everything in between.

Whether you need a more organized home or support through a life transition, we can tailor our help to what matters most to you.

01 / ORGANIZE

Home Organization

Organize closets, kitchens, garages, and living spaces. Create practical systems that make everyday life easier to manage.

02 / SIMPLIFY

Decluttering & Home Resets

Sort belongings, clear out crowded spaces, and refresh rooms with a thoughtful, judgment-free approach.

03 / RIGHT-SIZE

Downsizing

Make decisions about what to keep, gift, donate, or let go when preparing for a smaller or different space.

04 / COORDINATE

Moving Coordination

Organize timelines, to-do lists, vendors, and moving-day details to keep the process on track.

05 / SETTLE IN

Unpacking & Setup

Unpack, arrange furniture and belongings, and set up comfortable, functional spaces in a new home.

06 / SUPPORT

Family & Home Transition Support

Provide an extra set of hands for families managing a loved one's move, home projects, and all the details in between.

Meet the founder

Every home — and every next chapter — deserves care.

Hi, I'm the founder of Nest & Next Seattle. I started Nest & Next with a simple belief: life's transitions should feel less overwhelming and more manageable.

Whether you're looking for help organizing your home, preparing for a move, downsizing, or supporting a loved one through a transition, I understand there's much more involved than simply moving things from one place to another.

My approach is personal, organized, and hands-on. I bring structure to the process through thoughtful planning, project coordination, and practical support. Most importantly, I want every client and family to feel respected, supported, and confident throughout the process.
